ABOUT MASOODI EV MOTORS

Experience That Moves Forward.

Established in 1985, Masoodi EV Motors is an electric mobility business providing electric vehicles, battery solutions, EV spare parts and professional repair and maintenance services.

We serve individual customers as well as retailers, mechanics, workshop owners, EV dealers and wholesale buyers with reliable products and practical electric mobility solutions.
